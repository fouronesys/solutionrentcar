# Solutions Rent Car — Privacy Policy (template)

_Last updated: 2026-05-17_

Solutions Rent Car ("we", "our") provides this mobile application to let
customers and staff manage vehicle rentals. This page describes what we
collect and why.

## Data we collect

- **Account data**: name, phone, email, government ID and driver's license
  (only when you provide them during a booking).
- **Booking data**: vehicle reserved, dates, pick-up/return places, payments,
  signature.
- **Device data**: push notification token, OS/version, and app version, used
  exclusively to deliver booking and payment notifications.
- **Diagnostic data**: anonymous crash logs from Expo (no personal content).

## How we use it

- Operate the rental service: create and manage your bookings, process
  payments registered by our staff, deliver notifications.
- Comply with legal obligations (tax records, road authority requests).
- Improve the app via aggregate, anonymous analytics.

## Sharing

We do **not** sell personal data. We share data only with:
- Payment providers (where applicable, to register receipts).
- Expo (to deliver push notifications).
- Authorities, when legally required.

## Retention

We keep your booking history while your account is active and for as long as
local tax / commercial law requires.

## Your rights

You can request access, rectification or deletion of your data by writing to
**privacy@solutionsrentcar.com**.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
data from them.

## Changes

We will post any change to this policy here and update the date above.
