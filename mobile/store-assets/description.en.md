## Full description
Casa Rivas RentCar is the official mobile app of our vehicle rental company.
Book in seconds, get notified as your booking moves through each step, register
payments and keep your contact info up to date.

**For customers**
- Catalog of available cars with photos, prices and specs.
- Book by date, with pick-up and return location.
- "My bookings" with real-time status, registered payments and signature.
- Push notifications as your booking changes status.

**For staff**
- Today's agenda (deliveries and returns).
- Quick actions: mark delivered, mark returned, cancel booking.
- Register payments against any booking.
- Master booking list with filters by status, date and customer.

Bilingual Spanish/English with system-language detection.
